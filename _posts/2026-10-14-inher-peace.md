Am I seeking home, between her legs?  
Am I that type?

You know, mistaking lust at first sight for love eternal.

Am I an addict, bankrolling on emotional bankruptcy?

I leave pieces of my dysregulated self inside the women I use.  
I need their warmth: a cocoon for the void.

Incompatible but desperate to be intertwined.  
You know I only stayed because the sex was good.

She props me up after a pump.  
My muse, my mirror.  
I'll drop her suddenly.  
Then postscript her pain with performative self-pity.

Is it so wrong? 
To want happy ever after,  
the cardboard cutout kind?
  
I keep running, I can't stop.   
But I know I been had: two never become one.

My bad.

Inner peace, keep manifesting.

--- layout: page title: Wall permalink: /wall/ --- {%- comment -%} /wall/ is the wall: a free-form log book on the concrete. Anyone can write (on /wall/write/, wall-write.html), sign it with a name or not; each piece stays 3 days. No accounts, no profiles: only the words and the signature are kept. The writing lives with a small Cloudflare Worker (its code and setup: _tools/comment-wall/); `wall_api` in _config.yml is its address. While that's empty the page says the wall isn't open yet.
